# XD corpus coverage

What the 112 artboards in `design.xd` actually contain, which of them a golden
seed measures, and what is left.

Generated by clustering every artboard on the set of text strings it draws:
two boards carrying the same words are two **states of one surface**, not two
surfaces. Single-link at Jaccard ≥ 0.72 over the text sets, which collapses
112 boards into **68 distinct surfaces**.

    112 artboards → 68 surfaces → 39 seeded, 29 not
    80 of the 112 boards sit inside a seeded surface

Seeds outnumber seeded surfaces: `تفاصيل المتجر – 21` is one
surface carrying three seeds (the three tabs of طلباتي), and
`تفاصيل المتجر – 1` carries two, because the merchant's
preview of a storefront says the same things the customer's view of it does.

## Reproducing this

```bash
python tools/xd_reference/xd_reference_exporter.py \
  --xd design.xd --all-artboards --output-dir <dir> --skip-png
```

then cluster the exported SVGs on their text sets. The whole corpus exports
with `unsupported_node_counts == {}`, so nothing here is blocked by the
renderer.
